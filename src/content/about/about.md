building complex web applications with React and TypeScript.

building my own projects with PostgreSQL and TypeORM as I work toward becoming a
Full Stack Developer.

there is rarely a completely quiet day around here.

My ginger cat Ryzhulya has also become the heart of a small creative project I'm
building around her. I create cozy content featuring her and the other animals in
our little world, and I share it through Patreon for people who would like to
support them.

This website is where I share what I'm learning, the projects I'm building, and
the things I find worth writing down.
